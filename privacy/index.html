Privacy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Chimereader does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Your documents

Documents you open are processed on your device. They are not sent to us.

When the app uses the network

WhyWhat is sent
You paste a web link for it to readA request to the address you gave it.
The one-time download when you first open the appA request to the download host. No document content.

Purchases and the optional iCloud sync are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you turn on iCloud sync, your library is stored in your own iCloud account; we have no access to it.
