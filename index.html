Your documents, read aloud.

Open a PDF or EPUB — or paste a web link — and Chimereader starts reading in about a second — highlighting every word on the page as it goes. No conversion wait. No upload. No account.

Follow along, word by word

The highlight tracks the spoken word on the original page, so you can read with your eyes and ears together — or glance away and find your place again instantly.

It skips what you don't want read

Running headers, footers and page numbers are left out, so the reader never interrupts a sentence to say a page number. Equations, tables and charts are announced rather than read out as a stream of symbols.

Start anywhere

Jump to any page and listening begins there. Open a 600-page textbook, go to page 400, press play. There is no processing step to wait through.

PDFs, EPUBs and the web

Documents and ebooks read the same way. Paste a web article link and Chimereader reads that too.

Your documents stay yours

Reading and word alignment happen on your device. Your documents are never sent to our servers — there is no account and nowhere for them to go. Chimereader works in airplane mode.

Requirements. macOS 15 or later, iPadOS 18 or later, iOS 18 or later. One purchase covers Mac, iPad and iPhone.
