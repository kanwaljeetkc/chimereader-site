Age rating

Last updated 16 September 2026

Chimereader is rated 16+ because its web reader can open any web page.

Why 16+

You can paste a link to any article and have it read aloud. The App Store classifies that as unrestricted web access, which carries a 16+ rating, as web browsers do.

The app shows no advertising, has no chat or social features, and supplies no reading material of its own. See the privacy policy for how it handles data.
