Notes on keeping notes

Reading · September 2026

A note you never reread is a note you did not need to take

A note you never reread is a note you did not need to take. This sounds obvious and is almost universally ignored, which is why most note systems grow until they are too large to consult and are then abandoned.

The fix is to write notes addressed to a specific future reader: yourself, in six months, with no memory of the context. That reader needs the claim, the reason it mattered, and where it came from. They do not need your excitement.

Everything else is optional. A system that captures those three things and nothing else will be small enough to reread, which is the only property that matters.

The corollary is unwelcome. If a note cannot be written in that form, it is usually because the idea was not yet understood — and writing it down at length disguises that rather than fixing it.
